💡 Additional Recommendation for OmniDesk AI
Looking at your vision, I would add one more major module to the core application that many competitors lack:
Customer 360° Workspace
Instead of opening a customer profile in a simple side panel, create a dedicated Customer Workspace with tabs such as:
👤 Customer Overview
💬 Conversation History (across all channels)
📞 Calls & Voice Recordings
📧 Emails
📁 Shared Files
💰 Orders & Payments
📋 Support Tickets
📈 Lead & Deal Pipeline
📊 AI Insights
😊 Sentiment Timeline
⚠️ Risk Score
💡 Recommended Next Best Actions
📝 Internal Notes
🕒 Activity Timeline
This would become one of OmniDesk AI's strongest differentiators because every interaction with a customer is visible in a single intelligent workspace, giving agents complete context before they respond.

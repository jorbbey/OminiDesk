QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Do NOT design generic authentication screens.

The onboarding should feel effortless, personalized, and intelligent.

Every interaction should reduce friction.

The AI Setup Assistant should make users feel that OmniDesk AI understands their business from the first session.

The experience should maximize activation, reduce abandonment, and prepare users to immediately experience the core value of OmniDesk AI.

The final Figma project should be production-ready, enterprise-grade, visually polished, and ready for engineering handoff with minimal additional design work.
